How the charts were counted
Base
Each chart counts the parents who answered that question. Parents who said their kids do not play skipped everything after the first question.
Blanks
Blank answers are left out. A few parents skipped single rule questions, so those charts count slightly fewer people.
Games
Free-text game names were grouped by hand (for example PUBG and Pubjee, Free Fire and Freefire). “Don’t know”, “No idea” and “No” count as Don’t know.
Occupation
“Teacher” and “High school teacher” are counted together as Teacher.
